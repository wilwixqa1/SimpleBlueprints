THE BIG S106 LESSON (Will asked it directly: why don't tests catch these?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every consistency test verifies AGREEMENT. Both engines agreed level flush
sections get zero structure; the 3D faithfully drew nothing; parity stayed
green. Consistent wrongness is invisible to parity BY CONSTRUCTION. The
missing class is INVARIANTS against physics: test_structural_invariants.py
(I1 every section supported, I2 posts==footings, I3 freestanding rows
mirrored, I4 no post in a stair opening, I5 no unsupported beam segment).
Known failures live in EXPECTED_FAILURES entries naming their S107 owner;
stale entries (fixed but still registered) turn the suite red. Grow this
suite whenever a new "how did nothing flag this" moment occurs.
Second half of the answer: the golden has ZERO additive-section configs
(cutouts only). A pipeline that never renders sections in CI cannot regress
on them. Same class S102 fixed for freestanding.
